Teacher Aide Management System

This system is designed to manage teacher aide timetables and classroom assignments.

General Timetable

The General Timetable allows you to view the teacher aide timetable and access information about individual classes.

Teacher Aide Scheduling

Teacher aides can be assigned to classes through the class information window.

Schedule Search

The Teacher Aide Search allows you to search by day and period to see which teacher aides are scheduled and which aides are available.

Important

Current schedule information is stored while the webpage is open. Refreshing the webpage will keep the current teacher aide assignments.
